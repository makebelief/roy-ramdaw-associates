LABOUR & EMPLOYMENT LAW

Employment Contracts and Disciplinary Matters

Clear employment terms and fair workplace processes can prevent avoidable disputes. Roy Ramdaw & Associates Inc. lists employment contracts, disciplinary proceedings and workplace investigations among its labour services. The firm can review the relevant documents and advise on a proportionate response for an employer or employee.

← All practice areas

Employment contracts

Contracts may address duties, pay, working time, confidentiality, notice and dispute procedures. Their effect must be read with applicable legislation, collective agreements and workplace policies. The firm can review proposed terms or a disagreement about existing obligations and identify points needing clarification.

  • Check the signed contract and later variations
  • Compare relevant policies and collective agreements
  • Identify the disputed term and practical objective

Disciplinary processes

A disciplinary issue may call for investigation, notice of allegations, an opportunity to respond and a reasoned decision. The applicable requirements depend on the facts and current law. The firm can advise on preparation, records, procedural fairness and what steps may follow an outcome. It should not be assumed that every hearing allows an outside attorney to appear.

  • Keep notices, evidence and hearing records
  • Record dates and persons involved
  • Seek advice before a time-sensitive response or referral

Where a dispute follows

If a process ends in dismissal or another contested workplace decision, the forum and deadline depend on the claim. The firm can assess whether negotiation, an internal process, a bargaining council, the CCMA or litigation is appropriate.
