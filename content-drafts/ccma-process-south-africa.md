The Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA) is a statutory forum for many workplace disputes. A bargaining council may be the correct forum instead. The first practical question is therefore what dispute has arisen and which body has jurisdiction. The answer affects forms, deadlines, the available next step and who may represent a party.

## Referral and preparation

The CCMA's published rules explain how a party serves and files a referral, including proof that the other party received it. For an unfair dismissal claim, section 191 of the Labour Relations Act generally sets a 30-day referral period. Other dispute types have different limits. A late referral may require a condonation application. Check the relevant rule and date for the actual case before filing.

Prepare a short chronology and retain the employment contract, payslips, notices, correspondence and any hearing documents. State the dispute and desired outcome clearly. A referral form starts a process; it does not, by itself, establish that the claim will succeed.

## Conciliation

At conciliation, a commissioner helps the parties seek a resolution. The process is private and generally less formal than arbitration. A settlement can resolve the matter; if it remains unresolved, the commissioner may issue a certificate recording that result. Some cases use a combined conciliation-arbitration procedure, often called con-arb. Whether an objection to con-arb is possible depends on the dispute category and the rules.

## Arbitration or court

For disputes assigned to arbitration, a commissioner hears evidence and issues an award. Other disputes may proceed to the Labour Court after the required steps. Section 136 of the LRA describes a period for requesting arbitration after a certificate of non-resolution, subject to its terms and possible condonation. A Labour Court review of an award is a distinct process with its own rules and time limit.

Attorney representation is not automatic at every CCMA hearing. The rules particularly restrict legal representation in some dismissal arbitrations and may require agreement or permission. Before instructing an attorney to appear, confirm the case category and current rule.
