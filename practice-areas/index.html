EMPLOYMENT & CONTENTIOUS MATTERS

Employment and Labour Law — A Core Practice

Advice, negotiation, CCMA proceedings, bargaining council representation and litigation in the Labour Court and Labour Appeal Court.

01

Employment Disputes

Unfair dismissal, unfair labour practices, disciplinary disputes, workplace investigations, retrenchment and contractual employment matters.

02

Contingency Fee Matters

The firm considers contingency-fee instructions in qualifying employment disputes, personal injury, medical negligence and certain commercial disputes. Any arrangement is subject to the Contingency Fees Act and an agreed written mandate; outcomes are not guaranteed.

03

Litigation in All Court Levels

The practice reports litigation experience in South African courts, including the Constitutional Court, and direct High Court drafting and appearances by Dr Ramdaw where appropriate.
