# South African legal keyword strategy

**Research date:** 23 November 2026. This is an intent and page-mapping plan, not a volume or ranking report. No paid keyword database or authenticated Search Console data was available. A limited public search check for “labour lawyer Newcastle South Africa”, “unfair dismissal lawyer South Africa” and related CCMA queries showed local attorney listings, legal publishers, directories and official CCMA resources. That supports a **qualitative** view of competition only; it cannot establish search volume or ranking difficulty for each variant.

The firm's recorded head office is **29 Bird Street, Newcastle, KwaZulu-Natal**; a Durban office is also listed. Johannesburg is an **associated office**, not a firm branch. National service pages can discuss South African matters; only Newcastle is used as a primary location modifier. No fabricated location pages are proposed.

**Intent:** S = ready to enquire about a service; C = comparing counsel or representation; I = seeking information. **Competition:** N = broad national legal query, likely crowded based on the sampled national results; L = local query with attorney/directory competitors in the sampled Newcastle results; A = official source likely prominent for procedural information; U = variant not individually checked. These are research judgments, not keyword difficulty scores. **Priority:** P1 = direct, verified enquiry fit; P2 = relevant supporting intent; P3 = informational or ambiguous. Search Console should refine this after deployment.
