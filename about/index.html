ABOUT ROY RAMDAW & ASSOCIATES

A long-standing legal practice.

Headquartered in Newcastle, with a Durban office, a Johannesburg associated office and international professional connections.

OUR STORY

Practising since 1983.

Founded in 1983, Roy Ramdaw & Associates Inc. is headquartered at 29 Bird Street in Newcastle, KwaZulu-Natal, under Dr Anandroy Ramdaw, BProc, QLTT (London), LLD (Hon.).

The firm also works from Durban and through its associated Johannesburg office, Ramkussan Juglal Inc. Its present cross-border focus includes Power & Energy Law, finance for African projects, trusts and foundations, and Mauritius-linked offshore company structures. Mauritius and London connections are professional associations, not additional South African head offices.

Our aim is to present the firm's experience clearly and help visitors identify the appropriate point of contact for their matter.
