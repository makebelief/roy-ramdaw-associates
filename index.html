WELCOME TO OUR FIRM

Roy Ramdaw & Associates Inc.

A tradition of service. A broad legal perspective.

Established in 1983, the practice provides legal services across labour and employment disputes, commercial transactions, arbitration, property law and litigation.

Led by Dr Anandroy (Roy) Ramdaw, the firm has a published presence in Johannesburg, Durban and Newcastle, with international professional associations.

Committed to excellent service delivery in all fields of the law, locally and internationally.
