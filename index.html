WELCOME TO OUR FIRM

Roy Ramdaw & Associates Inc.

Newcastle roots. African and international perspective.

Headquartered at 29 Bird Street, Newcastle, the practice is led by Dr Anandroy Ramdaw, BProc, QLTT (London), LLD (Hon.), who has practised law since 1983.

The firm now places particular emphasis on Power & Energy Law, project finance transactions across Africa, cross-border arbitration and trusts, foundations and offshore corporate structures associated with Mauritius, alongside established litigation and employment law services.

Committed to excellent service delivery in all fields of the law, locally and internationally.
About Our Firm
A LONG-STANDING LABOUR LAW PRACTICE

Employment disputes. Strategic representation.

Dr Ramdaw has served as a CCMA commissioner, dealing with more than 2,500 labour disputes, and has acted as a judge in the Labour Court. The firm considers statutory-compliant contingency fee arrangements for eligible matters.
