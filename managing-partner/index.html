Meet Dr Anandroy (Roy) Ramdaw, director of Roy Ramdaw & Associates Inc.

DIRECTOR · ATTORNEY · CONVEYANCER · INTERNATIONAL ARBITRATOR

Dr Anandroy (Roy) Ramdaw

PRACTISING SINCE 1983

Dr Anandroy (Roy) Ramdaw is the director of Roy Ramdaw & Associates Inc., headquartered in Newcastle. His qualifications are BProc, QLTT (London) and LLD (Hon.), as listed in the firm’s updated professional profile.

Admitted as a South African attorney in 1983 and conveyancer in 1988, he has also been admitted as a solicitor in England and Wales, a barrister and solicitor in Australia, and a foreign lawyer in Mauritius. He has served as a CCMA-accredited commissioner and has been invited to act as a Labour Court judge.

His work includes labour law, complex litigation, international arbitration, Power & Energy Law and project finance across Africa. His Mauritius-related experience encompasses trusts, foundations, offshore corporate structures and associated international transactions.
